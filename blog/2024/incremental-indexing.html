How Incremental Indexing Works in Delta Sync

2024-12-05 · Engineering · 3 min read

Incremental indexing is the process of updating only the pages that have changed since the last crawl, rather than reprocessing the entire site. Delta Sync compares the lastmod timestamp in your sitemap against its internal index to decide which URLs need re-crawling.

When a page's lastmod value advances, Delta Sync fetches the new content, extracts text and metadata, and updates the index entry in place. Pages whose lastmod is unchanged are skipped entirely, keeping sync times proportional to the volume of changes rather than the total site size.

To get the most out of incremental indexing, keep lastmod accurate and avoid bumping it on pages that have not actually changed. False positives increase crawl load; false negatives cause stale index entries to persist until the next full resync.
