Understanding Sitemap-Driven Crawling

2024-09-20 · Engineering · 4 min read

Sitemap-driven crawling allows Delta Sync to discover and index all pages in a site without following hyperlinks. By reading sitemap.xml at the root of your site, the crawler builds a complete list of URLs to process.

Each URL entry supports lastmod, changefreq and priority attributes. Delta Sync uses lastmod to determine whether a page has changed since the last sync, making it highly efficient for large sites with infrequent updates.

For multi-level sites, we recommend grouping URLs by section and assigning appropriate priority values — 1.0 for the homepage, 0.8 for top-level sections, and 0.6 for deeper pages.
