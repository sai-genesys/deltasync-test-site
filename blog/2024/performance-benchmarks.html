2024-11-20 · Engineering · 5 min read

Following the release of Delta Sync v2.0 we ran a full benchmark suite across a range of file sizes and site depths. All tests were conducted on a single-core 2.4 GHz instance with a cold index (no prior sync state).

The gains in incremental sync are the most significant improvement in v2.0. By caching a content hash per URL, the engine can skip unchanged pages in under 1 ms each, making large sites with low churn extremely cheap to keep in sync.
